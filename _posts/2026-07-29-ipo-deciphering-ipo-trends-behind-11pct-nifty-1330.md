## What Could Go Wrong
As with any investment, there are risks associated with IPOs. The market can be volatile, and the IPO may not list at the expected price. There is also a risk of oversubscription, which can lead to allocation issues. Additionally, the company's financials and promoters' background may not be as strong as expected, which can impact the stock's performance in the long term. I'm cautious of the potential risks and am advising my clients to be selective and not get caught up in the hype. It's essential to do your research, evaluate the company's fundamentals, and consider the market sentiment before investing in an IPO.
