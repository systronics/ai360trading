### FAQ
1. What is the RSI 14 strategy that actually works on Nifty?
The RSI 14 strategy is a popular technical indicator that can be used to identify overbought and oversold conditions in the market. For Nifty, an RSI reading above 70 indicates overbought conditions, while a reading below 30 indicates oversold conditions.
2. How to use moving average crossover strategy 50 200 EMA?
The moving average crossover strategy involves using two moving averages with different time periods, such as 50-day and 200-day moving averages. When the 50-day moving average crosses above the 200-day moving average, it is a bullish signal, while a cross below is a bearish signal.
3. Does SIP timing matter if I invest for 15 years?
SIP timing does not matter if you invest for a long period, such as 15 years. The power of compounding will help you earn returns over the long term, regardless of the timing of your investments.
