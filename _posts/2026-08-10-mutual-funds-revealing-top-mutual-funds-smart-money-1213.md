### FAQ
1. What is the best way to invest in mutual funds, SIP or lump sum?
The best way to invest in mutual funds depends on your investment goals and risk tolerance. SIP is a good option for those who want to invest a fixed amount regularly, while lump sum is suitable for those who have a large amount to invest.
2. Which is better, index fund or active mutual fund?
Index funds and active mutual funds have their own advantages and disadvantages. Index funds offer broad market exposure at a low cost, while active mutual funds offer the potential for higher returns, but with higher costs and risks.
3. How do I evaluate a mutual fund's performance?
To evaluate a mutual fund's performance, you should consider factors like expense ratio, AUM, rolling returns, and the fund manager's track record.
