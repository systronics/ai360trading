## Best Case vs Worst Case
When evaluating investment options, it's essential to consider both the best-case and worst-case scenarios. For instance, investing in stocks can offer high returns, but it also comes with a higher risk of losses. On the other hand, investing in bonds or fixed deposits may offer lower returns, but they are generally considered safer. It's crucial to have a balanced portfolio that can weather both the best-case and worst-case scenarios.
