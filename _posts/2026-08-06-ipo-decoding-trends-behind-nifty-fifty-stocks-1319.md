## Trader FAQs
### How do I apply for an IPO online in India using ASBA or UPI step by step?
To apply for an IPO in India, you can use the ASBA or UPI method through your bank's online portal or a trading platform like Zerodha. The process typically involves selecting the IPO, entering the number of lots you wish to apply for, and authorizing the block amount. The application will be considered once the IPO opens for subscription.

### What is IPO GMP, and what are its real limits?
IPO GMP, or Grey Market Premium, is the premium at which the IPO is expected to list. However, it's essential to understand that GMP can be volatile and may not always reflect the actual listing price. The real limits of GMP lie in its unpredictability and the risk that the actual listing price may differ significantly.
