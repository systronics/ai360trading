## How It Affects Each Market
In the context of mutual funds and SIPs, each market has its unique characteristics and requirements. For instance, in India, investors often look for tax-saving options through ELSS (Equity-Linked Saving Scheme) funds, while in the US, index funds and 401(k) plans are popular choices. In the UK, investors may opt for ISA (Individual Savings Account) funds, and in Brazil, the focus is on finding the best index funds or actively managed funds that can navigate the local market conditions. Regardless of the market, the key to success lies in understanding the 5 key numbers that can help you pick mutual funds that consistently beat volatility. These numbers include the expense ratio, AUM (Assets Under Management), track record, fund manager's experience, and the standard deviation of the fund's returns.
