The best way to invest in mutual funds depends on the investor's risk profile and investment goals. However, SIP is generally considered a better option, as it helps to average out the cost of investment and reduces the risk of timing the market.
2. Which is better, index fund or active mutual fund? 
Index funds have consistently outperformed actively managed funds over the long term, and they offer a low-cost and diversified way to invest in the markets.

The key parameters to look at when evaluating a mutual fund are expense ratio, AUM, rolling returns, and manager performance.
