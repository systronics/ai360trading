Market Events

Quarterly results, dividends, bonus issues and splits for Nifty 200 stocks. These events move prices (sometimes a lot), so it helps to know them before you look at a chart.

UPDATED EVERY TRADING DAY{% if sl.generated_at %} · {{ sl.generated_at }}{% endif %}
What each event does to the price: Results can move a stock several percent either way, often more than any other event, and the direction is not known in advance. Dividend: on the ex-date the price simply drops by about the dividend (median dividend is under 0.5% of the price), nothing hidden. Bonus / split: the price falls overnight by the ratio (a 2-for-1 halves it) but your holding grows by the same ratio, so there is no real loss.

Honest note: knowing an event date is not a trading signal. In our own tests, buying the day after a bonus or split did not beat the market on average, and a dividend ex-date carries no hidden information. The one pattern we are still tracking is the few days before a result for stocks above their 50-day average, on a small sample, so we only watch it on paper.
