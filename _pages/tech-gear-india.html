Most phone and laptop problems have a simple cause. Here are the common ones, what usually helps, and what to check before you buy anything.

SSD for a slow laptop

Problem: laptop takes long to start and open apps

If your laptop still has an old hard disk, an SSD is often the biggest speed-up for the money. Check first that your laptop supports it and which type it needs (2.5-inch SATA or M.2).

  • Match the type your laptop supports
  • Note the capacity you need
  • Back up your data before changing the drive

External SSD or hard disk for backup

Problem: phone or laptop storage is full

Keep photos and important files in a second place so one failure does not lose everything. A small external drive is simple to use.

  • Prefer a drive with a USB-C or USB 3 connection
  • Keep two copies of anything important
  • Check the warranty period
See external drives on Amazon.in

Wi-Fi router for weak signal

Problem: Wi-Fi is slow or drops in some rooms

First move the router to a central, open place and restart it. If the signal is still weak in a large home, a better router or a mesh system may help.

  • Dual-band (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz) is a good starting point
  • Check your internet plan speed before buying
  • Ask your internet provider whether their router can be changed
See routers on Amazon.in

Laptop cooling pad

Problem: laptop gets hot or the fan is loud

Clean dust from the vents first, and do not use the laptop on a bed or blanket. A cooling pad can help some laptops during long use, but it is not a fix for a failing fan.

Power bank for low phone battery

Problem: phone battery does not last the day

A power bank is a simple backup when you are out. Check the capacity (mAh) and that it supports your phone's charging speed.

Pendrive for carrying files

Problem: need a quick way to carry or share files

A pendrive is fine for moving files, but do not treat it as your only copy. Buy from a seller you trust, since fake storage drives exist.
