How people use a list like this

  • Split the money. For example four equal parts over 3 to 6 months, so one bad entry day does not decide the result.
  • Add on weakness, not on hope. If a stock falls another 8 to 10% while its business is unchanged, the next part buys at a better average.
  • Prefer quality and liquidity. This list only includes liquid Nifty 200 names, but check the company's own results before buying.
  • Have an exit rule. Decide in advance what would make you stop adding, such as a broken business story or a price level you will not hold below.
Honest note: in our own 5-year test on 194 large stocks, buying after a big drop did not beat buying at random, and stocks can keep falling for months. This list is about disciplined, staggered accumulation of liquid stocks near support. It is not a promise that these prices are the bottom.
